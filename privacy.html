Privacy notice

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This is a short notice because we collect very little. This site has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies.

What the contact form collects

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, subject, and message. Those four fields are what we collect, and you choose what goes in them.

What happens to it

  • Your message is sent directly to the REACH Equity Lab inbox as an email. It is not written to a database or stored by this website.
  • Your email address is set as the reply address so we can respond to you.
  • The message is read by the members of REACH who handle correspondence.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your details with anyone else, and we do not add you to a mailing list from a contact form submission.

How long we keep it

Messages stay in our email account so we have a record of the conversation. If you would like your message and details deleted, email us and we will remove them.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and served over HTTPS. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard request information such as IP addresses in order to serve pages and protect the site against abuse. REACH does not receive or retain that information.

Links out

Pages on this site link to LinkedIn and to external repositories and journals. Once you follow one of those links you are on someone else's site, under their privacy policy rather than ours.

Contact

Questions about this notice, or a request to delete your data, can go to reachequitylab@gmail.com.

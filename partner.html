Partner with us

The most useful partnerships start with a decision someone actually has to make. If you have a question that open public data could help answer, or a finding you want tested in your setting, that is where we can be useful.

How we describe relationships

We use four distinct labels, and we apply them literally. Nobody is described as a partner on the strength of a meeting.

Relationship categories and what each requires
LabelDefinitionProof required
Community PartnerCo-designed or implemented an activity with REACHA written scope and the partner's permission to say so
Academic CollaboratorContributed scholarly expertise or infrastructureA documented contribution
SupporterGave funding or in-kind assistanceA financial or in-kind record
Institutional ResourceProvided an open opportunity or service we usedListed as a resource, never implied as a partnership

What we will not claim

  • REACH Equity Lab is a student organization at the University of Texas at Austin. We are not an institutional partner of the university, and we do not describe ourselves as one.
  • We do not display an organization's logo because we used its public data, attended its event, or contacted someone who works there.
  • We do not list prospective collaborations. A relationship appears here after it is agreed in writing, not while it is being discussed.

What a partnership involves

For an implementation pilot, we would work with you to define one problem, one setting, one decision-maker, one intervention, one implementation period, and one measurable adoption outcome. That scope is written down before work starts, so both sides know what finishing looks like.

For a research collaboration, we agree the question, the data sources, and the authorship expectations up front. Our analysis code and documentation are released publicly, so it is worth confirming early that this fits your constraints.

Start a conversation

Tell us the decision you are facing and the data you have access to. We will tell you honestly whether we are the right group for it.

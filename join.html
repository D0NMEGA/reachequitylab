Join REACH

There is more than one way in, and they ask for different amounts of time. General membership is open to eligible UT students. Research places are limited by project capacity, so they are selective, and the rubric we select on is written down and shared with applicants.

Levels of participation

  1. Public audience

    Events, the newsletter, and public resources. No membership required and nothing expected of you.

  2. General members

    Workshops, journal club, and volunteer activities. Open to eligible UT students. This is where most people start, and it is the best way to find out whether the research side suits you.

  3. Research fellows

    Selected into a defined sixteen-week project at 4 to 6 hours a week, with a faculty or senior adviser. Fellows carry a piece of real analysis from protocol through to a documented output.

  4. Project leads

    Manage a protocol, a team, and a set of deliverables. Leads are promoted from fellows who have completed a cohort.

  5. Directors

    Own a repeatable organizational system rather than a single project.

What we look for in fellows

Not prior research experience. The fellowship exists to teach that. What matters is that you can commit the hours through a full sixteen weeks, that you are willing to work carefully and slowly enough to document what you did, and that you are interested in the questions rather than in the line on a CV.

We are direct about this because the alternative wastes your semester: a fellowship place that goes unused is a project that does not finish, and a team of three or four people who are held up.

What you get

  • Four weeks of methods training embedded in a live project rather than taught in the abstract
  • A faculty or senior adviser on your project
  • Named contribution recorded against CRediT categories, with authorship decided on documented contribution
  • A public, citable output with your work in it, once it clears review

Applications

Fellowship applications open ahead of each cohort. Send us a message and we will tell you when the next round opens.

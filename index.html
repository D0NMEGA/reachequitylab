How REACH works

  1. Question

    A question that open public data can actually answer, written down as a protocol before any analysis begins.

  2. Evidence

    Analysis against a pre-specified plan, reviewed by someone who did not run it.

  3. Translation

    Findings rewritten for the people who would use them, with the code and documentation released alongside.

  4. Implementation

    Where a partner can use a finding, a scoped pilot measures whether it was adopted in practice.

Where we work

Three areas of health disparity research, studied with public and openly licensed data.

Racial gaps

Structural causes and outcomes of racial inequities in access and care quality.

Wage gaps

How income constraints shape care decisions, treatment adherence, and long-term outcomes.

Health gaps

Disparities in chronic conditions, prevention, and healthcare utilization.

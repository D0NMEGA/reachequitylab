Where we work

Three areas of health disparity research, studied with public and openly licensed data.

Racial gaps

Structural causes and outcomes of racial inequities in access and care quality.

Wage gaps

How income constraints shape care decisions, treatment adherence, and long-term outcomes.

Health gaps

Disparities in chronic conditions, prevention, and healthcare utilization.

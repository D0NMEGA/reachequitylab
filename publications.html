Conference presentations

Two abstracts have been accepted for poster presentation at the ISES 2026 Annual Meeting of the International Society of Exposure Science, held at the Sheraton Vancouver Wall Centre in Vancouver, British Columbia, from 4 to 8 October 2026. Both draw on the same analysis of NHANES biomonitoring data.

  1. Accepted · Poster

    Unsupervised Representation Learning Reveals Five Multi-Chemical Exposure Profiles in US Adults from NHANES

    Donovan Santine1 (presenting), Vibhu Kanna Rajesh Kanna1, Karthik Peravali1, Arjun Jaana1, Aryan Roghani1, Vy Nguyen2, Justin Colacino3

    A variational autoencoder reduces 472 chemical biomarkers measured in 52,063 NHANES participants between 1999 and 2018 to a 20-dimensional representation, which k-means clustering separates into five co-exposure profiles. The profiles are characterised without prior chemical selection, so the groupings come from the data rather than from a pre-chosen list of compounds.

  2. Accepted · Poster

    Data-Driven Multi-Chemical Exposure Phenotypes from 52,063 NHANES Adults Replace Default-Conservative Single-Compound Assumptions with Empirical Co-Exposure Profiles

    Donovan Santine1 (presenting), Vibhu Kanna Rajesh Kanna1, Karthik Peravali1, Arjun Jaana1, Aryan Roghani1, Vy Nguyen2, Justin Colacino3

    The same five phenotypes are used to ask a regulatory question: whether frameworks built around one compound at a time reflect the combinations of chemicals people are actually exposed to together.

1The University of Texas at Austin    2Silent Spring Institute    3University of Michigan

Publications and reports

REACH is building a rigorous research and open-science program. Completed publications, technical reports, datasets, and reproducible releases will appear here after public release.

What appears on this page

We keep a single public registry of completed outputs. An item is listed here only once it meets one of these conditions:

Public output registry rule
ListedNot listed
Published in a journal In development or analysis underway
Formally accepted Submitted to a journal
Released as a technical report Under peer review
Archived with a DOI A planned collaboration
Completed with partner permission Verbal interest only

This is why you will not find a list of projects in progress anywhere on this site. Naming work before it is finished creates an impression of output that does not exist yet, and it commits us publicly to findings that review may change.

Categories we will publish

  • Peer-reviewed publications — journal articles, listed with their DOI.
  • Evidence briefs — short summaries written for practitioners and policymakers.
  • Technical reports — full methods and results released directly by REACH.
  • Reproducible releases — the code and documentation behind a finding, archived with a version and DOI.
